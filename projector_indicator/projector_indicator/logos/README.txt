Logos for the advertisement card of the indicator projection (projector_indicator/ads.py).

Put a brand's official logo here as <brand>.svg (or .png / .webp / .jpg), e.g. porsche.svg, mcdonalds.svg.
Brands offer them in their press / media areas. Logos are the brands' trademarks: they stay out of the
repository (this folder is git-ignored except for this file). Without a file the card shows a plain badge.
Other texts use their letters and digits as name, e.g. 'Burger King' -> burgerking.svg.
